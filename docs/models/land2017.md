# Land2017

A different modeling paradigm: a human-ventricular contraction model built directly from
isometric tension measurements in human cardiomyocytes, combining troponin C / tropomyosin
kinetics with a three-state crossbridge cycle (unbound, pre-powerstroke, post-powerstroke) and a
distortion-decay model of crossbridge strain that captures the dynamic (velocity-dependent)
response to length changes. Length-dependent activation (LDA) here is *ad hoc*: two parameters
(`beta0`, `beta1`) shift, respectively, the maximum active tension and the calcium sensitivity
with sarcomere length.

[Lewalle2024](lewalle2024.md) amends this exact model by replacing `beta0`/`beta1` with explicit
myosin OFF-state force feedback.

**As an `.ode` file.**
[`land2017.ode`](https://github.com/ComputationalPhysiology/crossbridge/blob/main/src/crossbridge/land2017.ode),
reachable as `Land2017.ODE_FILE`, is this model as a gotranx file, written to the contract in
{ref}`ode-files`. `tests/test_ode_files.py` pins it to the class over a calcium twitch, with the
sarcomere shortened and relengthened, quickly released (so that $Z_s$ falls below $-1$), or held
at lengths that put $\Lambda$ below 0.87, on 0.87, between the two clamps, on 1.2 and above 1.2,
and once with its parameters off their defaults: the class's `Ta`, `Ka`, `bound_ca` and `Tp` at
1 ms steps agree with a tight `solve_ivp` solution of the file to 1e-4 of their peaks.
